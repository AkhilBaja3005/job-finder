# Cover Letter Crafting Skill

This skill teaches agents to write clean, concise, 3-paragraph cover letters that hiring managers actually read.

## Structure of an Effective Cover Letter

1. **Paragraph 1: The Hook & Alignment**
   - State the target role, where you saw it, and immediate alignment with the company's product mission. Avoid generic openings like *"I am writing to express my interest in..."*.

2. **Paragraph 2: The Core Evidence & Quantitative Impact**
   - Highlight 1-2 major technical achievements from your past roles that mirror the core requirements in the job description. Emphasize metrics, scale, and outcomes (e.g. latency reduced by 40%, revenue unlocked, systems scaled).

3. **Paragraph 3: Team Value Add & Call to Action**
   - Connect your technical toolkit to the challenges this team is solving over the next 12-18 months. Conclude with a confident, polite invitation to connect.

# Job Finder for Mac (first release)

A Mac app for the auto-apply part of Job Finder. Apple Silicon, not yet signed by Apple: the first time, right-click the
app and choose Open.

**What it does**
- Run: search by role and place, score jobs, tailor your resume, fill applications. Practice run is the default and never
  presses Submit.
- Applications: every job with its status. Mark replies, interviews and offers. Needs-review rows have Why and Review.
- Settings: AI keys (Gemini, Claude, OpenAI), your profile (build from a resume or import a profile.json), sign in to job
  sites once, save applications to your job-finder.space account with a sync code, optional Supabase of your own.
- A guided tour on first launch, a list of missing profile fields, daily application limit, a rough token count.

**Needs**: Google Chrome, and one AI key (Gemini has a free tier).

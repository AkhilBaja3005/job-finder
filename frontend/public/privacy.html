Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy covers the Job Finder website at https://www.job-finder.space and the Job Finder ATS Tailor Chrome extension. Job Finder scores job postings against your resume and helps you tailor applications.

What we collect

  • Account details. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address. We request no other Google data.
  • Your profile. The resume and profile details you enter or upload: contact details, work history, education, skills and links.
  • Job postings. The text of the job posting on the page you are viewing, when you use a feature in the extension or on the website.
  • Sync token. A token that links the extension to your account.

The extension reads a page only when you open it or use a feature, and form fields only when you click Auto-Fill. It does not track your browsing history.

How we use it

To score jobs, tailor resumes, draft cover letters and outreach, and fill application forms for you. To do this, the job posting text and the profile fields a feature needs are sent to our servers and to the language-model provider we use to generate the output. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it.

Storage and security

Your profile is stored in our database under your account so it syncs between the website and the extension. The extension also keeps settings and your profile in the browser's local storage. Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

Sharing

We share data only with the service providers needed to run Job Finder (hosting, database and the language-model provider). We do not transfer or use user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending, or for purposes unrelated to Job Finder's single purpose.

Retention and deletion

You can edit or delete your profile in the app. To delete your account and stored data, contact us using the details below. Uninstalling the extension removes the data stored in your browser.

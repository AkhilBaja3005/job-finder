   applications. Results, interviews and offers are not promised.
4. **AI output.** Cover letters, answers and tailored resumes are generated by AI models and can contain mistakes. Do not

5. **Your keys and costs.** If you use your own API key, the provider bills you for its use. The software shows a rough

6. **Your data.** See the privacy notice. You can export or delete your account at any time.
7. **Acceptable use.** Do not use the software to apply under a false identity, to flood employers, or to break the law.

8. **Availability.** The hosted server may be down or change. The software is provided as is, without warranty, to the
   extent the law allows.
